# What Meddleware is

Meddleware makes open versions of tools and services that usually come from companies that want to keep you. The tools available
now are the first; more are on the way, and they won't all be about the same thing.

## What every tool promises

- **You can leave.** Everything you put in, you can take out, in open formats, without asking us. [Leaving with your data](/start/leaving)
  says how, tool by tool.
- **It works with others.** We use open standards, not private formats, so our tools connect to other people's and theirs to ours. The
  developer site lists [every standard we use](https://meddleware.dev/platform/standards).
- **Nobody is watching.** No adverts, no tracking, no selling data, no cookies unless you sign in. We earn from published fees and
  nothing else. [How we treat your data](/start/your-data) has the detail.
- **You can run it yourself.** Everything is open source (0BSD). Use ours, or host your own copy.

## Why some tools use a blockchain

Some of the tools use a public blockchain, because there it lets you stop trusting us: a pass you buy sits in your wallet, a payment
goes through rules anyone can read, and we cannot take either back. Where a blockchain does not do that for you, a tool will not use one.
Each tool's guide says what it runs on.
